The Anti-Grammarly

We are tired of overly polished AI-generated emails. You know the kind—stuffed with em-dashes and phrases that scream "an AI wrote this".

betterr is built as an "anti-Grammarly". It is an AI tool designed to help you write emails that sound like an actual human wrote them. Sometimes that means leaving in subtle imperfections, drastically shortening phrases, and removing corporate filler.

Subtle

Eliminates filler words and shortens phrases without losing your original voice.

Human

Goes a step further by making your message genuinely conversational and grounded.

CEO

Frames your message to the point. Like tech CEOs communicating in under 10 words.

"True human writing isn't always perfectly polished. And honestly, we'd rather read messages like that over the tons of AI-generated DMs we get every day."

How to Install betterr

1

Download the Extension

Click below to download the official betterr extension ZIP file to your computer.

Download .zip
2

Open Extensions Page

In Chrome, type chrome://extensions in your URL bar and hit Enter.

3

Enable Developer Mode

Toggle the "Developer mode" switch in the top right corner of the extensions page to ON.

4

Load Unpacked

Extract the downloaded betterr-extension.zip file. Then, click the Load unpacked button and select the extracted folder.
